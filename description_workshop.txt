[h1] UI Editor – Customize Your Game ✨ [/h1]  
Want a cleaner, more personal setup? With **UI Editor** you can drag, drop, and move all the game menus exactly where you want them.  
Hotbar, chat, map, health, mana, buffs - arrange everything to fit your style.  

[h2] 🌟 Drag & Drop UI [/h2]  
Move everything with your mouse and lock in your style. Your layouts save automatically, and you can even share your favorite layouts with your friends! 🤝  

[h2] 🌟 Resource Pack Toggler [/h2]  
Enable or disable resource packs instantly while in a world – no restart needed. ⚡
